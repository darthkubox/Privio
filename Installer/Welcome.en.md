# Privio - Privacy. Locked by you.

Privio protects selected apps on your Mac with **Touch ID or your password**. When a protected app becomes active, Privio hides it immediately and asks for authentication - and only restores it after you unlock.

It also locks apps after a period of inactivity and after sleep or screen lock, and everything runs **locally**: no cloud, no accounts, no telemetry.

## Important scope of protection

Privio primarily protects an application's interface. It does not lock or encrypt that application's
files, databases or backups. It is an additional privacy layer - not a substitute for screen lock, a
strong password, FileVault, updates, backups or physical control of the Mac - and it cannot provide
100% data protection. The Private Vault encrypts only content placed directly in the vault by the
user.

This installer will place Privio in your **Applications** folder and guide you through accepting the license and authorizing with an administrator.
